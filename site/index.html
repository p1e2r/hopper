Mods, packs and shaders, sorted.

Hopper is a free, open-source Minecraft mod manager that latches onto the Minecraft folder you already have. Pick your version and loader, grab things from Modrinth or drop in your own files, and switch anything on or off.

What it does

Uses your folder

Finds your .minecraft folder on its own. No instances, no accounts, no launcher.

Right version, every time

Pick your Minecraft version and Fabric, Quilt, Forge or NeoForge. Every download, dependencies included, matches it.

Recognises your mods

Files you already have are matched on Modrinth and shown by their real names, with links.

On/off switches

Turn mods, resource packs and shaders off without deleting them. Removed files go to the trash.

Modrinth built in

Search and install mods, resource packs and shaders in one click.

Your own files too

Drop .jar and .zip files on the window and Hopper sorts them into the right folder.
